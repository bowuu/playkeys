About Playkeys

Independent Manifest V3 extension built for privacy, performance, and media control.

Principle of Least Privilege

Playkeys only requests access to https://app.plex.tv/* by default. Custom local and remote server origins are only requested on-demand when explicitly configured by you.

Zero-Data Collection & Privacy

Playkeys does not collect, record, track, transmit, or monetize any personal information, media history, or browsing activity. All state synchronizations operate strictly locally between your active browser tabs and the background worker.

Toolbar Squircle Badge

The badge dynamically indicates playback state (▶ for playing, ⏸ for paused) and completely clears whenever playback finishes, stops, or your Plex tab closes.
